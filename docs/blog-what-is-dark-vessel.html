In short

暗船（dark vessel）是關閉 AIS、卻仍被合成孔徑雷達（SAR）衛星偵測到的船。 關閉 AIS 不代表船消失——SAR 仍能在黑暗與雲層中拍到金屬船體。將 SAR 偵測與 AIS 交叉比對， 「有雷達、沒 AIS」的目標即為暗船。暗船是值得研判的線索，並非違法認定。

A dark vessel is a ship that has switched off its AIS transponder yet is still detected by Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) satellites. Going dark does not make a vessel disappear — SAR still sees the metal hull through darkness and cloud. Cross-referencing SAR detections with AIS, a "radar yes, AIS no" target is a dark vessel. It is a lead to investigate, not a legal finding.

一艘船在台灣海峽上行駛，原本每隔幾秒就向全世界廣播自己的位置。某個時刻，訊號突然中斷——它從所有航運追蹤網站上消失了。

但它並沒有真的消失。在 600 公里高空，一顆雷達衛星仍清楚地看見它。這就是「暗船」。

暗船是什麼？

「暗船」（dark vessel）指的是被衛星雷達偵測到、卻沒有對應 AIS 訊號的船隻。它的船體確實在海面上、被雷達拍到，但在一般依賴 AIS 的追蹤平台上卻「看不見」。

AIS（自動識別系統）是大型船舶依規定主動廣播的無線電訊號，包含船名、位置、速度與航向。當一艘船關閉 AIS，業界稱為「走暗」（going dark）。走暗之後，它就成了 AIS 世界裡的隱形人——但物理世界裡，它仍是一塊在水面移動的大型金屬。

A ship sails across the Taiwan Strait, broadcasting its position to the world every few seconds. Then, at some moment, the signal cuts out — it vanishes from every shipping-tracker website.

But it has not really vanished. 600 km overhead, a radar satellite still sees it clearly. That is a "dark vessel."

What Is a Dark Vessel?

A "dark vessel" is a ship that is detected by satellite radar but has no matching AIS signal. Its hull is genuinely on the water and visible to radar, yet it is "invisible" on the AIS-based tracking platforms most people use.

AIS (Automatic Identification System) is the radio signal large ships are required to broadcast, carrying name, position, speed, and course. When a vessel switches off AIS, the industry calls it "going dark." Once dark, it becomes invisible in the AIS world — but in the physical world it is still a large piece of metal moving across the sea surface.

暗船如何被偵測？

關鍵在於把兩種不同的「看船」方式疊在一起：

📡
第一層：AIS船舶主動廣播的數位身分與位置。優點是資訊豐富，缺點是可以被關閉或偽造。
🛰️
第二層：SAR 衛星合成孔徑雷達主動發射微波、接收回波，直接「看見」水面上的金屬船體，無法被船上關閉。

把同一時段的兩層資料交叉比對，就會出現三種情況：

  • SAR 有偵測 + 有 AIS 訊號 → 一般船隻
  • SAR 有偵測 + 無 AIS 訊號 → 暗船（Dark Vessel）
  • 無 SAR 偵測 + 有 AIS 訊號 → 雷達當下未掃到（或非金屬小艇）

第二種情況，就是本站優先標記與追蹤的對象。

How Are Dark Vessels Detected?

The trick is to overlay two completely different ways of "seeing" a ship:

📡
Layer 1: AISThe digital identity and position a ship broadcasts itself. Rich in detail — but can be switched off or faked.
🛰️
Layer 2: SAR satellitesSynthetic Aperture Radar emits microwaves and reads the echoes, directly "seeing" the metal hull on the water — something the crew cannot turn off.

Cross-reference both layers for the same time window and three cases appear:

  • SAR detection + AIS signal → Normal vessel
  • SAR detection + no AIS signal → Dark Vessel
  • No SAR detection + AIS signal → radar simply didn't pass over it (or a small non-metal craft)

The second case is exactly what this site flags and tracks first.

AIS 關閉不代表船消失

這是關於暗船最常見的誤解。很多人以為「船從追蹤網站上不見了，就是駛離了海域」——其實不然。

AIS 只是一個自願廣播的無線電應答器。關掉它，就像把手機關機：你聯絡不到那個人，但那個人並沒有從地球上消失。船依然在原處航行、依然會撞上海底電纜、依然會在公海與另一艘船旁靠。

為什麼這很重要：正因為關閉 AIS 不會讓船真的消失，獨立的衛星偵測（SAR）才成為關鍵——它讓「走暗」這個動作本身變成可被看見、可被記錄的證據，而不是一片空白。

Switching Off AIS Doesn't Make a Ship Disappear

This is the most common misconception about dark vessels. Many assume that "if a ship drops off the tracker, it has left the area." It hasn't.

AIS is only a voluntary radio transponder. Switching it off is like powering down a phone: you can no longer reach that person, but they have not vanished from the planet. The ship is still sailing where it was, can still snag a submarine cable, and can still rendezvous with another vessel on the high seas.

Why this matters: precisely because going dark does not actually remove the ship, independent satellite detection (SAR) becomes decisive — it turns the act of "going dark" itself into visible, recordable evidence rather than a blank gap.

SAR 衛星與 Global Fishing Watch 的角色

合成孔徑雷達（SAR, Synthetic Aperture Radar）是一種主動式雷達衛星。它自己發射微波、再接收從地表與海面反射回來的回波，因此不依賴陽光，也能穿透雲層——白天、黑夜、陰雨都能成像。金屬船體對雷達的回波特別強，在 SAR 影像上會呈現明亮的亮點。

本站使用的 SAR 暗船偵測結果來自 Global Fishing Watch（全球漁業觀察）。他們處理歐洲太空總署 Sentinel-1 等 SAR 衛星影像，自動辨識海面上的船舶亮點，再與公開 AIS 資料比對，標出沒有 AIS 匹配的目標。本站每 12 小時擷取一次這些結果，疊加到台灣周邊海域地圖上。

SAR 為什麼難以被欺騙 AIS 可以關閉、可以偽造座標；但 SAR 是從太空直接量測物理回波。船想要不被 SAR 看見，唯一的方法是不要出現在水面上——這對一艘真正在執行任務的船而言並不可能。

The Role of SAR Satellites and Global Fishing Watch

Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) is an active radar satellite. It emits its own microwaves and reads the echoes bouncing back from land and sea, so it does not depend on sunlight and can see through clouds — imaging by day, by night, and in rain. Metal hulls return especially strong echoes and appear as bright spots in SAR imagery.

The SAR dark-vessel detections used here come from Global Fishing Watch. They process SAR imagery from satellites such as the European Space Agency's Sentinel-1, automatically identify vessel bright-spots on the sea surface, compare them against public AIS data, and flag the targets with no AIS match. This site pulls those results every 12 hours and overlays them on a map of the waters around Taiwan.

Why SAR Is Hard to Fool AIS can be switched off or spoofed; SAR measures a physical echo directly from space. The only way for a ship to avoid being seen by SAR is to not be on the water surface at all — impossible for a vessel actually carrying out a mission.

台灣周邊暗船活動為什麼重要？

台灣是一座島，對外的網際網路、金融與通訊幾乎完全仰賴海底電纜。一旦關鍵電纜被切斷，影響的不只是上網，而是整個社會的運作韌性。

近年多起海底電纜受損事件中，反覆出現一個令人警覺的模式：涉案船隻會在接近電纜前關閉 AIS，使其在破壞行為發生時無法被即時追蹤與攔截。這正是暗船監測的核心價值——把「走暗」這個刻意隱匿的動作，重新攤在陽光下。

🔌
海底電纜威脅在電纜路線附近走暗、低速徘徊或拖錨，是最高警戒的行為組合之一。
🔄
海上旁靠與走私關閉 AIS 進行船對船轉運，規避港口檢查，常見於制裁規避與石油走私。
🐟
非法漁業越界或在禁漁區作業時走暗，隱匿真實作業位置。
🔍
灰色地帶與偵察在敏感海域進行偵察或施壓時關閉 AIS，刻意維持模糊與可否認性。

Why Dark-Vessel Activity Around Taiwan Matters

Taiwan is an island whose internet, finance, and communications depend almost entirely on submarine cables. If a key cable is severed, the impact is not just slower browsing — it is the resilience of an entire society.

Across several recent cable-damage incidents, one alarming pattern keeps recurring: the vessels involved switch off AIS before approaching the cable, so they cannot be tracked or intercepted in real time while the damage occurs. This is the core value of dark-vessel monitoring — taking the deliberately concealed act of "going dark" and putting it back in plain sight.

🔌
Submarine cable threatGoing dark, loitering at low speed, or dragging anchor near a cable route is one of the highest-alert behavior combinations.
🔄
STS transfer & smugglingDisabling AIS for ship-to-ship transfers to evade port inspection — common in sanctions evasion and oil smuggling.
🐟
Illegal fishingGoing dark while fishing across borders or in restricted zones to hide the true operating location.
🔍
Gray zone & reconnaissanceSwitching off AIS during reconnaissance or coercion in sensitive waters to preserve ambiguity and deniability.

本專案如何判讀暗船？

本站不會把「暗船」直接等同於「壞船」。我們把暗船當作一個起點，再用一套透明的規則去判斷它的風險高低：

  1. 從 Global Fishing Watch 取得 SAR 偵測，標出無 AIS 匹配的暗船位置。
  2. 把暗船與已知的 AIS 軌跡、海底電纜路線、漁撈熱點與領海基線疊圖比對。
  3. 檢查行為特徵：是否在電纜 5 公里內、是否低速徘徊、是否之字形航行、是否曾頻繁走暗或變更身分。
  4. 依 8 項指標累計威脅分數，並按船舶類型加權（貨輪、油輪權重高，漁船權重低）。
  5. 分數達門檻者標記為「可疑」，列入監測清單供人工進一步研判。

完整的評分邏輯、門檻與限制，公開記載於 本站方法論；即時的暗船分布可在 暗船偵測地圖 查看。

How This Project Reads Dark Vessels

This site does not treat "dark vessel" as a synonym for "bad ship." We treat a dark vessel as a starting point, then apply a transparent set of rules to judge how risky it is:

  1. Obtain SAR detections from Global Fishing Watch and mark dark vessels with no AIS match.
  2. Overlay them against known AIS tracks, submarine cable routes, fishing hotspots, and territorial baselines.
  3. Check behavioral features: within 5 km of a cable, loitering at low speed, zigzag course, a history of going dark or changing identity.
  4. Accumulate a threat score across 8 criteria, weighted by vessel type (cargo and tanker weigh more; fishing less).
  5. Vessels reaching the threshold are flagged "suspicious" and listed for further human assessment.

The full scoring logic, thresholds, and limits are documented openly in our methodology; the live dark-vessel distribution is on the dark-vessel map.

資料限制與免責聲明

  • SAR 偵測代表「雷達可見的目標」，不必然代表違法活動；目標也可能是合法商船、漁船或公務船。
  • 關閉 AIS 在某些情況（如海盜區安全考量）屬合法，走暗本身不等於違規。
  • SAR 衛星只在通過時拍攝，覆蓋有時間與空間間隙；沒被偵測不代表該處無船。
  • AIS 資料可能因轉發延遲、覆蓋缺漏或設備問題而不完整，影響「有無匹配」的判定。
  • 本站的可疑分數是風險排序，用於輔助人工研判，並非法律認定，也不指控任何具名船隻違法。

Data Limitations & Disclaimer

  • A SAR detection means a "radar-visible target" and does not by itself indicate illegal activity; it may be a lawful merchant ship, fishing vessel, or government vessel.
  • Switching off AIS is lawful in some situations (e.g. safety in piracy areas); going dark is not itself a violation.
  • SAR satellites image only as they pass over, leaving spatial and temporal gaps; no detection does not mean no ship was present.
  • AIS data can be incomplete due to relay delay, coverage gaps, or equipment issues, affecting the "match / no match" determination.
  • Our suspicious score is a risk ranking to assist human analysis — not a legal finding, and not an accusation against any named vessel.

FAQ

What is a dark vessel?
A dark vessel is a ship detected by Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) satellites that has no matching AIS signal. It is genuinely on the water and visible to radar, but "invisible" on the AIS trackers most people use.
Does a ship disappear after switching off AIS?
No. AIS is just a radio signal the ship broadcasts itself; turning it off does not remove the ship from radar. SAR satellites still detect the metal hull on the water, regardless of weather, cloud, or time of day.
How are dark vessels detected?
SAR satellites scan the sea to obtain vessel positions, which are cross-referenced against concurrent AIS data. A SAR-detected target with no AIS match is flagged as a dark vessel. This site uses Global Fishing Watch's SAR detections.
Does being flagged as a dark vessel mean it is illegal?
Not necessarily. A SAR detection means a radar-visible target — possibly a fishing, merchant, or government vessel — and switching off AIS is lawful in some cases. A dark vessel is a lead to investigate, not a legal determination.
Why does dark-vessel activity around Taiwan matter?
Taiwan depends heavily on submarine cables for external connectivity, and cable-damage incidents often show a "switch off AIS before approaching the cable" pattern. A dark vessel that appears near a cable route and loiters at low speed is a behavior our threat scoring specifically watches for.
