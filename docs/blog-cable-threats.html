🌊 多重威脅的交匯

海底電纜的敵人，不只有大魚和船錨。在台灣海峽的海底深處，這些細如手臂的光纖束正面對著有史以來最複雜的威脅組合——自然的、意外的，以及最令人不安的，蓄意的。

一條跨洋海底電纜的主體，直徑往往不過數公分，比一般人的手臂還細。然而，台灣超過 95% 的對外網際網路流量、跨國金融交易、政府通訊與軍事資料，都依賴這些埋在數百公尺乃至數千公尺深海底的脆弱光纖。當其中一條斷裂，影響可能只是網速變慢；但當多條同時失效，整座島嶼可能瞬間進入「數位孤島」狀態。

過去，海底電纜的威脅模型相對單純——主要來自地震、洋流與漁船作業等可預期的因素。但近年來，一種全新的威脅樣態正在浮現：以民用船隻為掩護、刻意切斷關鍵基礎設施的「灰色地帶」攻擊。這類行動介於和平與戰爭之間，難以歸因、難以起訴，卻能造成真實的傷害。

本文將威脅分為三大類——自然威脅、意外損壞、蓄意灰色地帶攻擊——逐一拆解其機制、頻率與偵測難度，並透過波羅的海的真實案例，理解台灣正在面對的，究竟是什麼樣的對手。

🌊 A Convergence of Threats

The enemies of a submarine cable are not only big fish and ship anchors. Deep beneath the Taiwan Strait, these arm-thin bundles of optical fibre now face the most complex combination of threats in history — natural, accidental, and most disturbingly, deliberate.

The core of a transoceanic submarine cable is often only a few centimetres across — thinner than a human arm. Yet more than 95% of Taiwan's international internet traffic, cross-border financial transactions, government communications and military data all depend on these fragile fibres buried hundreds or even thousands of metres below the surface. When one is severed, the impact may be no more than slower speeds; but when several fail at once, an entire island can be plunged into a "digital island" state.

In the past, the threat model for submarine cables was relatively simple — earthquakes, currents and fishing operations, all reasonably predictable. In recent years, however, a wholly new threat has emerged: "gray zone" attacks that use civilian vessels as cover to deliberately sever critical infrastructure. Such operations sit between peace and war — hard to attribute, hard to prosecute, yet capable of real harm.

This article divides the threats into three categories — natural, accidental, and deliberate gray zone attacks — dissecting the mechanism, frequency and detectability of each, and uses real cases from the Baltic Sea to understand exactly what kind of adversary Taiwan now faces.

🌋 自然威脅

大自然始終是海底電纜最古老、也最不留情的對手。台灣位處環太平洋地震帶與多條海底峽谷之上，地質活動頻繁，海床地形劇烈起伏，本身就是高風險環境。

最具代表性的案例發生在 2006 年 12 月 26 日。當天，恆春外海發生規模 7.0 的強震，震央位於巴士海峽附近的海底。這場地震引發了大規模的海底山崩與濁流（turbidity current），如同水下的雪崩，沿著海底峽谷以高速向下衝刷，一舉切斷了通過該海域的 7 條海底電纜。台灣對外通訊瞬間癱瘓，東亞地區的網路與電話服務大面積中斷，金融交易停擺，修復工作耗時數週才完全恢復。這起事件至今仍是研究海底電纜地震風險的經典教材。

除了地震，自然威脅還包括：

颱風與海上惡劣天候：強烈颱風會攪動淺海海床、改變沉積物分布，並使修復船無法出海作業，間接延長斷纜的影響時間。

海底山崩與濁流：即使沒有地震，陡峭海底峽谷邊緣的沉積物也可能因重力失穩而崩落，形成挾帶大量泥沙的高速水流，足以扯斷或掩埋電纜。

深海洋流與底質磨蝕：長期的強勁底層洋流會使懸空的電纜段不斷摩擦海床岩石，造成外被層磨損，最終導致疲勞性斷裂。這類損壞往往緩慢而隱蔽，難以即時察覺。

自然威脅的共通特徵是：頻率相對可預測、可透過路線選擇與工程設計（如深埋、加裝鎧裝）降低風險，且歸因明確——沒有人會懷疑一場 7.0 地震是「敵對行為」。這與後文將討論的蓄意攻擊形成鮮明對比。

🌋 Natural Threats

Nature has always been the oldest and most merciless adversary of submarine cables. Taiwan sits atop the Pacific Ring of Fire and several submarine canyons, with frequent geological activity and dramatically uneven seabed terrain — a high-risk environment in itself.

The most representative case occurred on 26 December 2006. A magnitude 7.0 earthquake struck off the coast of Hengchun, with its epicentre on the seabed near the Bashi Channel. The quake triggered massive undersea landslides and turbidity currents — like underwater avalanches — that scoured down the submarine canyons at high speed and severed seven submarine cables passing through the area in one blow. Taiwan's international communications were instantly paralysed; internet and telephone services across East Asia were widely disrupted, financial transactions halted, and full repairs took weeks. The incident remains a classic case study in submarine cable seismic risk.

Beyond earthquakes, natural threats include:

Typhoons and severe weather: Powerful typhoons stir up shallow seabeds, shift sediment distribution, and keep repair ships in port — indirectly prolonging the impact of a break.

Undersea landslides and turbidity currents: Even without an earthquake, sediment at the steep edges of submarine canyons can fail under gravity, forming high-speed sediment-laden flows powerful enough to snap or bury a cable.

Deep-sea currents and abrasion: Sustained strong bottom currents cause suspended cable spans to rub continuously against seabed rock, wearing away the outer layers until fatigue fracture occurs. Such damage is often slow and hidden, difficult to detect in real time.

The common feature of natural threats is that their frequency is relatively predictable, the risk can be reduced through routing and engineering (deep burial, armoured cable), and attribution is clear — no one suspects a magnitude 7.0 quake of being a "hostile act." This stands in sharp contrast to the deliberate attacks discussed below.

⚓ 意外損壞

若論斷纜事件的數量，意外損壞才是真正的「常客」。根據國際電纜保護委員會（ICPC）與全球統計，人為意外是海底電纜故障的最大宗來源。台灣每年平均發生 7 至 9 次斷纜，其中絕大多數並非蓄意，而是來自漁業與航運活動。

從成因分布來看，台灣周邊與全球的斷纜統計大致呈現以下比例：

  • 41% 來自漁撈作業——尤其是底拖網漁船
  • 18% 原因不明——無法確切判定，部分可能是隱蔽的蓄意行為
  • 16% 來自船錨拖曳——商船在錨地或淺水區下錨或走錨
  • 9% 來自敵對第三方——可疑或蓄意行為

底拖網漁船的機制：深海底拖網作業會將沉重的網板（otter board）與配重沉到海床，沿海底拖行數公里以捕撈底棲魚類。當拖網路徑橫越未深埋的電纜時，網板可能直接鉤住、磨破或扯斷電纜。台灣海峽水淺、漁業活動密集，正是這類意外的高發區。

船錨拖曳的機制：大型商船的錨重達數噸，錨鏈長達數百公尺。當船隻在錨地下錨後因風浪或操作不當而「走錨」（dragging anchor），拖行的錨爪會在海床上犁出一道溝痕，任何位於其路徑上的電纜幾乎無一倖免。一艘貨輪若以低速在電纜路線上方拖錨數公里，造成的破壞與蓄意攻擊在物理上幾乎無法區分——這正是灰色地帶攻擊得以「藏身」於意外之中的關鍵。

2023 年，台灣全年發生多達 12 次斷纜，遠高於 7 至 9 次的歷史平均，已讓各界警覺異常。而到了 2025 年 1 至 2 月，短短兩個月內就發生 4 次斷纜——這樣的密集程度，已經很難單純用「意外」來解釋。

⚓ Accidental Damage

If counted by number of incidents, accidental damage is the real "regular visitor." According to the International Cable Protection Committee (ICPC) and global statistics, human accidents are the single largest source of submarine cable faults. Taiwan averages 7 to 9 breaks per year, the vast majority of which are not deliberate but arise from fishing and shipping activity.

By cause, breaks around Taiwan and worldwide roughly follow this distribution:

  • 41% from fishing operations — especially bottom trawlers
  • 18% unknown — cannot be precisely determined; some may be covert deliberate acts
  • 16% from anchor dragging — merchant ships anchoring or dragging in anchorages and shallow water
  • 9% from hostile third parties — suspicious or deliberate acts

The mechanics of bottom trawling: Deep-sea bottom trawling drags heavy otter boards and weights along the seabed for kilometres to catch demersal fish. When the trawl path crosses a cable that is not deeply buried, the boards can hook, abrade or snap it. The shallow, fishing-dense Taiwan Strait is a hotspot for such accidents.

The mechanics of anchor dragging: A large merchant ship's anchor weighs several tonnes, with chains hundreds of metres long. When a ship "drags anchor" after anchoring — due to wind, waves or poor handling — the dragging fluke ploughs a furrow across the seabed, and almost no cable in its path survives. A cargo ship dragging its anchor for kilometres above a cable route at low speed causes damage that is physically almost indistinguishable from a deliberate attack — which is precisely how gray zone attacks "hide" within accidents.

In 2023, Taiwan recorded as many as 12 breaks in a single year, far above the historical average of 7 to 9, raising alarm. Then in January–February 2025, four breaks occurred in just two months — a density that is hard to explain by "accident" alone.

🕵️ 蓄意灰色地帶攻擊

灰色地帶攻擊的精髓，在於「可否認性」。攻擊者不派遣軍艦、不發射飛彈，而是利用看似平凡的民用商船，以「意外走錨」為掩護，達成切斷關鍵基礎設施的戰略目的。即使被當場逮獲，肇事者也能聲稱「只是漁船作業」或「錨機故障」，讓對方難以追究。

影子船隊的戰術手法：

1. 關閉 AIS（走暗）：船舶自動識別系統（AIS）是全球船隻定位的基礎，但它可以被人為關閉。當一艘船在接近電纜路線時關閉 AIS，便等於從監控雷達上「消失」，事後極難重建其行蹤。

2. 權宜船籍（旗幟便利國）：船隻刻意懸掛與實際所有人、營運者毫無關聯的國家旗幟——如坦尚尼亞、多哥等。這些「旗幟便利國」（flags of convenience）監管寬鬆，使追查真正幕後者極為困難。

3. 異常徘徊與低速拖行：在電纜路線上方反覆低速航行、之字形繞行或長時間停留，正是本監測站威脅評分系統著重偵測的行為特徵。

宏泰 58（Hong Tai 58）：2025 年 2 月，一艘懸掛多哥旗幟的貨輪涉及台灣海域電纜損壞事件，再次呈現權宜船籍＋異常航行的典型影子船隊樣態。

這些案例之所以難以起訴，正是因為它們完美地遊走在「意外」與「攻擊」的灰色地帶。而要識破它們，唯一的方法就是長期、系統性地監測異常船舶行為——這正是本站存在的理由。

🕵️ Deliberate Gray Zone Attacks

The essence of a gray zone attack is "deniability." The attacker sends no warships and fires no missiles; instead it uses ordinary-looking civilian merchant vessels, cloaked as "accidental anchor dragging," to achieve the strategic goal of severing critical infrastructure. Even if caught in the act, the culprit can claim it was "just fishing" or "an anchor winch malfunction," making accountability nearly impossible.

Shadow fleet tactics:

1. Switching off AIS (going dark): The Automatic Identification System (AIS) is the foundation of global vessel positioning, but it can be turned off. When a ship disables AIS while approaching a cable route, it effectively "vanishes" from surveillance, making its movements nearly impossible to reconstruct afterwards.

2. Flags of convenience: Vessels deliberately fly the flag of a state with no connection to the real owner or operator — such as Tanzania or Togo. These flag-of-convenience states have lax oversight, making it extremely hard to trace who is truly behind the ship.

3. Abnormal loitering and slow dragging: Repeatedly sailing slowly above a cable route, zigzagging, or lingering for long periods — exactly the behavioural signatures this monitor's threat-scoring system is built to detect.

Xing Shun 39: In January 2025, a cargo ship flying the Tanzanian flag loitered abnormally near a cable route off northern Taiwan with its AIS switched off. By the time coast guard vessels arrived, the cable was severed and the ship was speeding away.
Hong Tai 58: In February 2025, a cargo ship flying the Togolese flag was implicated in cable damage in Taiwanese waters — once again the classic shadow-fleet pattern of flag-of-convenience plus abnormal navigation.

These cases are so hard to prosecute precisely because they walk perfectly along the gray line between "accident" and "attack." The only way to expose them is long-term, systematic monitoring of abnormal vessel behaviour — which is exactly why this site exists.

🧭 波羅的海的警示

台灣並非孤例。在地球另一端的波羅的海，一連串幾乎相同的事件，為台灣提供了清晰的預警。

易鵬 3 號（Yi Peng 3）：2024 年 11 月，一艘中國籍散裝貨輪「易鵬 3 號」在波羅的海被指控以拖曳船錨的方式，切斷了連接瑞典與立陶宛、以及芬蘭與德國之間的兩條海底電纜。調查顯示，該船在斷纜時段內以異常路徑緩慢航行，疑似拖錨橫越電纜。多國海軍隨即對其進行包圍與監控，但由於該船懸掛的旗籍國擁有專屬管轄權（詳見下一篇執法框架文章），實際的法律追究困難重重。

俄羅斯影子船隊的模式：波羅的海事件的背景，是俄羅斯為規避制裁而建立的龐大「影子船隊」——大量老舊、保險不明、船籍可疑的油輪與貨輪。這些船隻在運送被制裁石油之餘，也被懷疑兼作破壞海底基礎設施的工具。其手法包括走暗、權宜船籍、異常徘徊——與台灣海域所見如出一轍。

北京的鏡像戰術：令人警惕的是，2025 年初台灣周邊的興順 39、宏泰 58 等事件，幾乎是波羅的海劇本的「東亞翻版」。同樣的權宜船籍、同樣的走暗、同樣的拖錨疑雲，顯示中國正在學習並複製這套低成本、高否認性的灰色地帶手法，用以測試台灣的偵測能力與反應底線。

波羅的海的教訓很清楚：當這類事件從「偶發」變成「頻發」，就不再是巧合，而是一種有計畫、可重複、難歸因的混合戰策略。台灣若不及早建立系統性的監測與證據鏈，將永遠處於「事後才知道」的被動。

🧭 The Baltic Warning

Taiwan is not alone. On the other side of the planet, a series of nearly identical incidents in the Baltic Sea provides a clear early warning.

Yi Peng 3: In November 2024, the Chinese-flagged bulk carrier Yi Peng 3 was accused of severing two submarine cables in the Baltic — one between Sweden and Lithuania, and one between Finland and Germany — by dragging its anchor. Investigations showed the ship sailing slowly along an abnormal path during the break window, apparently dragging its anchor across the cables. Several navies surrounded and shadowed the vessel, but because its flag state holds exclusive jurisdiction (see the next article on enforcement), actual legal accountability proved extremely difficult.

The Russian shadow-fleet pattern: The backdrop to the Baltic incidents is the vast "shadow fleet" Russia built to evade sanctions — large numbers of aging tankers and cargo ships with unclear insurance and suspicious registries. Beyond carrying sanctioned oil, these ships are also suspected of doubling as tools to damage undersea infrastructure. Their methods — going dark, flags of convenience, abnormal loitering — are exactly what is seen in Taiwanese waters.

Beijing's mirroring tactics: Alarmingly, the early-2025 incidents around Taiwan — Xing Shun 39, Hong Tai 58 — are almost an East Asian remake of the Baltic script. The same flags of convenience, the same going dark, the same anchor-dragging suspicions show that China is learning and replicating this low-cost, highly deniable gray zone playbook to test Taiwan's detection capability and response thresholds.

The lesson of the Baltic is clear: once such incidents shift from "occasional" to "frequent," they are no longer coincidence but a planned, repeatable, hard-to-attribute hybrid-warfare strategy. Unless Taiwan builds systematic monitoring and a chain of evidence early, it will remain forever in the passive position of "only finding out after the fact."

🤖 新興威脅

威脅並未停留在拖錨與底拖網的時代。隨著科技演進，幾種更隱蔽、更難防範的新興威脅正逐漸成形。

深海電纜切割裝置：有研究與專利顯示，已出現可在數千公尺深海運作的拖曳式電纜切割工具。相較於傳統拖錨的「粗暴」，這類裝置能更精準地針對特定電纜下手，且更容易偽裝成正常的海床作業，大幅提升歸因難度。

自主水下載具（AUV）：無人化的水下機器人可在不需母船持續停留的情況下，潛入深海執行偵察、標定甚至破壞任務。由於沒有船員、沒有 AIS、體積小不易被雷達或衛星偵測，AUV 幾乎是灰色地帶攻擊的「完美工具」——一旦被廣泛運用，傳統以水面船舶行為為核心的監測方法將面臨嚴峻挑戰。

未來的混合戰：可以預見，未來的海底基礎設施攻擊將不再是單一手法，而是多種威脅的組合：以民用商船製造「意外」掩護、以 AUV 執行精準破壞、以網路攻擊干擾修復調度、以資訊戰否認與混淆。台灣面對的，是一個不斷進化、刻意模糊和平與衝突界線的對手。

面對這樣的威脅光譜，單靠工程加固（深埋、鎧裝、多路由備援）已不足夠。真正的韌性，必須建立在「即時偵測 + 證據保全 + 國際協作」三位一體的能力之上——而這也正是本系列下兩篇文章將深入探討的主題。

🤖 Emerging Threats

Threats have not stayed in the age of anchors and bottom trawls. As technology advances, several stealthier and harder-to-counter threats are taking shape.

Deep-sea cable-cutting devices: Research and patents indicate the existence of towed cable-cutting tools capable of operating at depths of thousands of metres. Compared with the "brute force" of anchor dragging, such devices can target specific cables more precisely and are more easily disguised as normal seabed work — sharply raising the difficulty of attribution.

Autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs): Unmanned underwater robots can dive into the deep sea to conduct reconnaissance, marking and even sabotage without a mother ship lingering on the surface. With no crew, no AIS, and a small profile hard to detect by radar or satellite, an AUV is almost the "perfect tool" for gray zone attacks — and if widely deployed, traditional monitoring methods centred on surface-vessel behaviour will face severe challenges.

The future of hybrid warfare: Future attacks on undersea infrastructure will foreseeably no longer rely on a single method, but on a combination: civilian merchant ships to manufacture "accidental" cover, AUVs for precision sabotage, cyberattacks to disrupt repair coordination, and information warfare to deny and confuse. Taiwan faces an adversary that is constantly evolving and deliberately blurring the line between peace and conflict.

Against such a spectrum of threats, engineering hardening alone (deep burial, armouring, multi-route redundancy) is no longer enough. True resilience must rest on a trinity of "real-time detection + evidence preservation + international cooperation" — exactly the themes the next two articles in this series explore in depth.
