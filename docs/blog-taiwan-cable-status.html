台灣的戰略位置與電纜密度

台灣位於亞太地區海底電纜的十字路口。從日本、韓國向南延伸至東南亞、澳洲，以及連接美洲的跨太平洋幹線，大量電纜必須繞過或途經台灣周邊海域。台灣海峽雖然平均水深僅約 60 公尺（屬淺海），但正因如此，電纜更容易暴露在漁業活動與船錨的威脅之下。

台灣本島目前有 14 條國際海底電纜登岸（包括台北縣八里、頭城、台南、高雄等登陸站），連接美國、日本、香港、菲律賓、關島等地。這些電纜是台灣對外通訊、金融交易、半導體訂單的實體基礎。

Taiwan's Strategic Position and Cable Density

Taiwan sits at the crossroads of Asia-Pacific submarine cables. Cables running from Japan and South Korea southward to Southeast Asia and Australia, as well as transpacific trunk routes to the Americas, must skirt or pass through Taiwan's surrounding waters. The Taiwan Strait averages only about 60 meters in depth — shallow water that makes cables far more vulnerable to fishing activity and anchors.

Taiwan's main island currently has 14 international submarine cables landing at stations in Bali (New Taipei), Toucheng, Tainan, and Kaohsiung, connecting to the US, Japan, Hong Kong, the Philippines, Guam, and beyond. These cables are the physical backbone of Taiwan's external communications, financial transactions, and semiconductor order flows.

外島：最脆弱的環節

台灣本島的 14 條國際電纜提供了一定的冗餘——即使幾條同時中斷，其他電纜仍可承接流量。但外離島的情況截然不同。

⚠️ 馬祖列島
馬祖位於台灣海峽北部，距中國福建沿岸僅 10 公里，是台灣最前線的外離島。全島僅靠 2 條海底電纜（PCCS 與 MATSU-T）與台灣本島連接。

2023 年 2 月，這兩條電纜在 6 天內相繼被中國漁船切斷。馬祖 1.4 萬居民的網路完全中斷，只能依靠頻寬極為有限的微波通信維持基本連線。中華電信緊急啟用 Starlink 衛星服務作為臨時替代，但頻寬不足的問題依然嚴重。修復歷時約 50 天。
⚠️ 金門縣
金門位於台灣海峽南部，距中國廈門僅 10 公里。目前僅有 1 條主電纜加 1 條備援電纜。雖然較馬祖略好，但一旦兩條同時中斷，金門同樣將陷入通訊孤立。
🟡 澎湖縣
澎湖群島距台灣本島較近，擁有相對較多的電纜連接，韌性優於馬祖與金門，但在整個台灣外島體系中仍是弱環。

Outer Islands: The Most Vulnerable Links

Taiwan's main island has 14 international cables providing redundancy — if a few are cut simultaneously, others can absorb the traffic. But the outer islands face a very different situation.

⚠️ Matsu Islands
Matsu lies in the northern Taiwan Strait, only 10 km from China's Fujian coast — Taiwan's most frontline outer island. The entire archipelago depends on just 2 submarine cables (PCCS and MATSU-T) to connect with Taiwan's main island.

In February 2023, both cables were severed by Chinese fishing vessels within 6 days. Internet connectivity for Matsu's 14,000 residents was completely cut, with only extremely limited microwave communications remaining. Chunghwa Telecom activated emergency Starlink service as a temporary workaround, but bandwidth was severely insufficient. Repairs took approximately 50 days.
⚠️ Kinmen County
Kinmen lies in the southern Taiwan Strait, 10 km from China's Xiamen. It currently has 1 main cable plus 1 backup. While slightly better than Matsu, if both are simultaneously cut, Kinmen would face the same communication isolation.
🟡 Penghu County
The Penghu archipelago is closer to Taiwan's main island and has relatively more cable connections, giving it better resilience than Matsu or Kinmen — though still a weak point in Taiwan's outer island connectivity.

斷纜趨勢：數字會說話

海底電纜損壞本是全球性的常態現象——每年全球約 100–200 起，多數源自漁業和船錨。但台灣周邊海域的特殊地緣環境，使斷纜頻率遠超應有的比例。

Break Trends: The Numbers Tell a Story

Submarine cable damage is a global norm — roughly 100–200 incidents per year worldwide, mostly from fishing and anchors. But Taiwan's unique geopolitical environment means break frequency far exceeds what geography alone would predict.

修復的挑戰：不只是等待那麼簡單

海底電纜修復是一項極為專業、耗時且昂貴的工程。全球專業海纜維修船（Cable Repair Ship）僅約 60 艘，分佈在各大洋，往往需要數日才能抵達出事海域。

修復一條電纜需要什麼？
  • 派出專用海纜維修船（日租金數萬美元）
  • 精確定位海底斷點（透過時域反射計 TDR 和 ROV 潛水器）
  • 將電纜打撈至水面（深海區難度極高）
  • 在特製接頭艙（joint box）中完成光纖熔接
  • 完成修復後放回海底，並測試訊號品質
整個過程視水深與損壞程度，通常需要 14–60 天。2023 年馬祖斷纜，修復費時約 50 天。

更棘手的是：如果多條電纜同時受損（如 2006 年恆春地震），有限的維修船隻必須排隊——等待修復的時間可能長達數月。這正是「灰色地帶攻擊」同時針對多條電纜的場景，所能造成的最大損害。

The Repair Challenge: More Than Just Waiting

Submarine cable repair is an extraordinarily specialized, time-consuming, and expensive undertaking. There are only about 60 dedicated cable repair ships in the world, distributed across the oceans, often requiring days just to reach the incident site.

What Does Repairing a Cable Require?
  • Deploy a dedicated cable repair ship (daily charter costs tens of thousands of dollars)
  • Precisely locate the break on the seabed (using Time Domain Reflectometry and ROV submersibles)
  • Raise the cable to the surface (extremely difficult in deep water)
  • Splice fiber optics in a specialized joint box
  • Return the repaired cable to the seabed and test signal quality
The full process typically takes 14–60 days depending on depth and damage extent. The 2023 Matsu outage took about 50 days to repair.

More challenging still: if multiple cables are simultaneously damaged (as in the 2006 Hengchun earthquake), the limited pool of repair ships must queue — wait times can stretch to months. This is precisely the maximum-damage scenario of a gray zone attack targeting multiple cables at once.

政府因應：韌性提升的努力

2023 年馬祖事件後，台灣政府開始更積極地正視海底電纜脆弱性。目前已採取或規劃的措施包括：

  • NCC（國家通訊傳播委員會）推動電信業者建立備援方案，包括衛星通訊（Starlink）作為緊急備份
  • 推動外島第三條電纜計畫（馬祖、金門）
  • 2023 年修訂《電信管理法》第 72 條，明訂損壞海底電纜者可處 3 年以下有期徒刑
  • 強化海巡署在電纜路由周邊的巡邏密度
  • 與國際海纜產業組織（ICPC）加強情報共享
問題的根本：即使建設了第三條電纜，只要目前的執法困境與旗國主義漏洞未能解決，蓄意破壞者仍可以重複同樣的手法。硬體冗餘只能降低單一事件的衝擊，無法阻止敵對行為本身。這是第 05 篇〈執法框架與挑戰〉將深入探討的核心問題。

Government Response: Resilience-Building Efforts

Following the 2023 Matsu incident, Taiwan's government has moved more actively to address submarine cable vulnerability. Measures taken or planned include:

  • NCC (National Communications Commission) pushing telecom operators to establish backup plans, including satellite communications (Starlink) for emergency use
  • Promoting third cable projects for outer islands (Matsu, Kinmen)
  • 2023 amendment to Telecommunications Management Act Article 72, stipulating imprisonment up to 3 years for damaging submarine cables
  • Strengthening Coast Guard patrols near cable routes
  • Enhanced intelligence sharing with the International Cable Protection Committee (ICPC)
The fundamental problem: Even with a third cable installed, as long as the enforcement gap and flag-state jurisdiction loophole remain unresolved, deliberate saboteurs can repeat the same tactics. Hardware redundancy reduces the impact of any single incident, but cannot prevent the hostile behavior itself. This is the core issue explored in depth in Article 05: Enforcement Framework and Challenges.

登陸台灣的國際海纜與「登陸站」

台灣的對外連通並非平均散布，而是高度集中在少數登陸站（landing station）。國際海纜在這些站點上岸、與陸地骨幹網路相接。公開資料顯示，台灣本島的主要登陸站集中在北部的頭城、淡水與南部的枋山一帶。

多條主要國際海纜系統在此交會，把台灣接上日本、香港、東南亞與跨太平洋的網路。問題在於：當登陸站與電纜路徑在地理上彼此靠近，單一地點或單一海域的事件，就可能同時影響多條電纜。

這種「地理集中」是效率與脆弱的一體兩面。它讓建設與維運更有效率，卻也意味著少數關鍵節點承擔了不成比例的風險——尤其當這些海域同時是繁忙的漁場與航道時，要區分「意外」與「蓄意」就更加困難。

外島的處境更為極端：連接馬祖、金門等外島的海纜數量本就稀少，缺乏足夠的替代路徑。一旦受損，當地對外通訊可能在修復前長時間仰賴微波或衛星等備援，頻寬與穩定度都大打折扣。

The International Cables Landing in Taiwan — and Its "Landing Stations"

Taiwan's external connectivity is not spread evenly; it is highly concentrated in a handful of landing stations, where international cables come ashore and connect to the terrestrial backbone. Public records show Taiwan's main landing stations clustered around Toucheng and Tamsui in the north and Fangshan in the south.

Several major international cable systems meet here, linking Taiwan to Japan, Hong Kong, Southeast Asia, and across the Pacific. The catch: when landing stations and cable routes sit geographically close together, an event at a single site or in a single patch of sea can affect multiple cables at once.

This geographic concentration is two sides of one coin — efficiency and fragility. It makes construction and operations more efficient, but it also means a few critical nodes carry a disproportionate share of the risk — and when those waters are also busy fishing grounds and shipping lanes, separating "accident" from "intent" becomes harder still.

The outer islands are even more exposed: the cables serving Matsu, Kinmen and other offshore islands are few to begin with, with little redundant routing. Once damaged, local external communications may rely for a long time on microwave or satellite backup until repair — at a heavy cost in bandwidth and stability.

常見問題 FAQ

為什麼馬祖斷網特別嚴重？
馬祖全島僅靠 2 條海底電纜連接台灣本島。2023 年 2 月這兩條電纜在 6 天內相繼被中國船隻切斷，1.4 萬居民斷網長達約 50 天。
海底電纜斷了要修多久？
視水深與損壞程度，通常需 14 至 60 天。2023 年馬祖斷纜修復約 50 天，因為全球專業海纜維修船僅約 60 艘，往往須排隊。
台灣海底電纜斷裂頻率正常嗎？
全球每年約 100–200 起斷纜，但台灣周邊因地緣因素比例偏高。2023 年達 12 起，2025 年僅 1、2 月就發生 4 起。
台灣有多少條國際與國內海纜？
台灣本島有 14 條國際海底電纜及約 10 條國內海纜，後者連接馬祖、金門、澎湖等外離島。

FAQ

Why was the Matsu internet outage so severe?
Matsu depends on just 2 submarine cables to the main island. In February 2023 both were cut by Chinese vessels within 6 days, leaving 14,000 residents offline for about 50 days.
How long does it take to repair a cut cable?
Typically 14–60 days depending on depth and damage. The 2023 Matsu repair took about 50 days, since only roughly 60 specialized cable repair ships exist worldwide and they often queue.
Is Taiwan's cable break frequency normal?
Globally there are ~100–200 breaks a year, but Taiwan's waters see a disproportionate share — 12 incidents in 2023, and 4 in just January–February 2025.
How many international and domestic cables does Taiwan have?
Taiwan's main island has 14 international submarine cables and about 10 domestic cables connecting outer islands like Matsu, Kinmen, and Penghu.
