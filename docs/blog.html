📚 About This Knowledge Hub

Taiwan's submarine cables are more than technical infrastructure — they are the digital lifeline on which this nation's existence depends. From the Matsu cable cut to shadow fleet operations in the Taiwan Strait, submarine cables face unprecedented gray zone threats. This knowledge hub integrates technical explainers, geopolitical analysis, and monitoring methodology to help readers understand this complex issue from multiple angles.

Whether you're a researcher interested in maritime security, a citizen concerned about Taiwan's situation, or a technical user wanting to understand this site's monitoring methods — you'll find in-depth, trustworthy reference material here. All articles are available in both Traditional Chinese and English.
