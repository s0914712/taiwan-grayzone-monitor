LAW OF THE SEA 01
什麼是「海洋權益」？
What Are "Maritime Rights"?
主權／主權權利／管轄權三層結構
Sovereignty, Sovereign Rights & Jurisdiction
《海洋基本法》所稱的「海洋權益」不是單一權利，而是主權、主權權利、管轄權三個強度遞減的層次，對應國家安全、海域治安與海事安全。海底電纜的保護就鑲嵌在這個結構裡。
"Maritime rights" under Taiwan's Ocean Basic Act is not one right but three tiers — sovereignty, sovereign rights, jurisdiction — mapping onto national security, law-and-order, and maritime safety. Cable protection sits inside this structure.

LAW OF THE SEA 02
海底電纜的國際法保護
Cables Under International Law
從 1884 公約到 UNCLOS
From the 1884 Convention to UNCLOS
1884 公約最有牙齒卻少國批准，UNCLOS 第 113 條只給「立法管轄」、不給「執行管轄」。再加上船旗國專屬管轄，受害國經常落入「有法律、卻動不了那艘船」的困境。
The 1884 Convention has the most teeth but few parties; UNCLOS Art. 113 grants only prescriptive, not enforcement, jurisdiction. With exclusive flag-state jurisdiction, the victim state often has "a law but no power to touch the ship."
