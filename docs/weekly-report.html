What this page shows

威脅評分引擎每天標出上千艘高風險船（score ≥8），單日快照看不出趨勢。本頁把它們按 ISO 週與日曆月彙整： 互動地圖畫出海纜旁 0.1° 低速徘徊熱區（滯留總時數、涉及船數、平均船速）與高風險船最後位置； 逐船列出船種、船籍、出港／進港港口、海上時間、海纜滯留時數與滯留期間平均船速， 每期附 CSV／JSON 原始檔可直接下載作為報告素材。

The scoring engine flags hundreds of high-risk vessels (score ≥8) every day; single-day snapshots hide the trend. This page aggregates them by ISO week and calendar month: an interactive map shows 0.1° low-speed loitering hotspots near submarine cables (total loiter hours, distinct vessels, average speed) and last-known high-risk vessel positions; the per-vessel table lists type, flag state, departure/arrival ports, time at sea, and average speed while loitering near cables. Each period ships raw CSV/JSON downloads.

The per-vessel detail keeps the top 400 vessels per day by priority (critical → cable loitering → offshore loitering → score); departure/arrival ports are inferred from AIS tracks against known port areas (all of Taiwan; Fujian / southern Zhejiang / eastern Guangdong for China) — "no port call observed" does not mean none occurred.
