What this page shows

本頁顯示 SAR（合成孔徑雷達）衛星偵測到、但沒有對應 AIS 訊號的「暗船」。 關閉 AIS 不代表船隻消失，仍可能被雷達拍到。SAR 偵測代表雷達可見目標，不必然代表違法活動。

This page shows "dark vessels" — targets detected by SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellites that have no matching AIS broadcast. Switching off AIS does not make a vessel disappear from radar. A SAR detection indicates a radar-visible target, not necessarily illegal activity.

  • 暗船 ＝ SAR 偵測到、卻無 AIS 匹配的目標。
  • Dark vessel = a SAR-detected target with no AIS match.
  • SAR 暗船分析每 12 小時更新，資料來自 Global Fishing Watch。
