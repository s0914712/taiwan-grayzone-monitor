In short

海事制裁是各國政府與國際組織對特定船舶、船東與航運公司施加的限制，用來切斷受制裁對象的海運與金流。被指定的船舶會以其 IMO 編號列入 OFAC、EU、UK、UN、UANI 等公開名單。受制裁的「影子船隊」油輪則以換旗、改名、假 IMO 與走暗來規避——而用公開名單比對即時 AIS，就會發現這些船其實常出現在台灣周邊海域。

Maritime sanctions are restrictions that governments and international bodies place on specific ships, owners, and shipping companies to cut off a target's shipping and money flows. A designated vessel is listed by its IMO number on public lists such as OFAC, EU, UK, UN, and UANI. Sanctioned "shadow-fleet" tankers evade this by hopping flags, changing names, broadcasting fake IMOs, and going dark — yet matching public lists against live AIS shows these ships regularly appear in Taiwan's waters.

海事制裁是什麼？

「海事制裁」是指各國政府或國際組織，對特定船舶、船東、經理人、航運或保險公司所施加的限制措施。目的通常是切斷某個受制裁對象（例如特定政權、核擴散或走私網絡）用來運輸貨物與取得金流的海運管道。

一旦一艘船被「指定」（designate），各國的企業與金融機構原則上就禁止與它交易——不得提供保險、加油、船級驗證、港口服務或金流結算。被指定的船通常會以其船舶識別碼列入公開名單，其中最關鍵的就是 IMO 編號。

A tanker flies the flag of Sierra Leone, broadcasts an invalid IMO with an extra digit, and the name it announces is not the name on the sanctions list.

It is not hiding from pirates — it is hiding from a government list that carries its IMO number.

What Are Maritime Sanctions?

"Maritime sanctions" are restrictions that governments or international bodies impose on specific ships, owners, managers, and shipping or insurance companies. The aim is usually to cut off the shipping and money flows a sanctioned target (a regime, a proliferation or smuggling network) uses to move goods.

Once a ship is "designated," companies and banks are in principle barred from dealing with it — no insurance, bunkering, classification, port services, or payment settlement. A designated ship is typically listed by its vessel identifiers, of which the most important is the IMO number.

誰在制裁？名單怎麼運作？

沒有單一的「全球制裁名單」。實務上有多個機構各自維護清單，一艘船可能同時被好幾個機構指定：

🇺🇸
OFAC（美國財政部）維護 SDN（特別指定國民）名單，是全球影響力最大的制裁清單，被列入者幾乎被隔絕於美元金融體系之外。
🇪🇺
EU（歐盟）透過理事會決議指定船舶與實體，並由各成員國執行；EU Sanctions Map 提供公開查詢。
🇬🇧
UK OFSI（英國）維護金融制裁綜合名單，脫歐後獨立於 EU 運作。
🇺🇳
UN 安理會透過決議（如對北韓的 1718 制裁）指定船舶，具國際法效力，但船隻數量相對少。
🌐
其他瑞士 SECO、紐西蘭 MFAT、澳洲等亦有清單；非政府組織 UANI 則彙整涉伊朗石油的油輪。OpenSanctions 等平台會把這些來源整合供查詢。
為什麼是 IMO 編號船名、呼號、甚至 MMSI 都可以更改，唯有 IMO 編號設計上「終身不變」，跟著船體而非跟著登記。因此制裁名單以 IMO 指定，比對時用 IMO 才可靠——這也是為什麼有些船會刻意廣播無效或造假的 IMO。

Who Sanctions, and How Do the Lists Work?

There is no single "global sanctions list." In practice several bodies each maintain their own, and one ship may be designated by many at once:

🇺🇸
OFAC (U.S. Treasury)Maintains the SDN (Specially Designated Nationals) list, the most influential sanctions list in the world; a listing all but cuts a ship off from the US-dollar financial system.
🇪🇺
EU (European Union)Designates vessels and entities via Council decisions, enforced by member states; the EU Sanctions Map offers public lookup.
🇬🇧
UK OFSIMaintains the consolidated financial-sanctions list, operating independently of the EU since Brexit.
🇺🇳
UN Security CouncilDesignates vessels via resolutions (e.g. the 1718 regime on North Korea) with force in international law, though the vessel counts are relatively small.
🌐
OthersSwitzerland's SECO, New Zealand's MFAT, and Australia keep lists too; the NGO UANI compiles tankers tied to Iranian oil. Platforms like OpenSanctions consolidate these sources for lookup.
Why the IMO numberName, call sign, even MMSI can be changed; only the IMO number is designed to be permanent, tied to the hull rather than the registration. Sanctions lists therefore designate by IMO, and matching is reliable only by IMO — which is exactly why some ships deliberately broadcast an invalid or fabricated IMO.

受制裁的船怎麼規避？

被指定後，這些船（多屬「影子船隊」）會用一整套手法讓自己「查不到、賴不掉」：

🏴
換掛權宜船旗在被某個船旗國除籍後，數天內就改掛獅子山、葛摩、喀麥隆等管理寬鬆的登記國。
🆔
改名、改 MMSI切斷與舊紀錄的連結。有的船 AIS 廣播的名字，與制裁名單上登記的名字不同——這種「名不符實」本身就是強烈的身分掩蓋訊號。
🔢
假 IMO 編號廣播格式錯誤（如多一碼）或造假的 IMO，讓自動化名單比對失效。
📡
走暗與 AIS 欺騙在裝卸或敏感海域關閉 AIS、或廣播假位置。
🔄
海上旁靠（STS）在外海把制裁油轉運到另一艘「乾淨」的船，切斷貨物來源，避開進港受檢。

How Do Sanctioned Ships Evade?

Once designated, these ships (mostly part of a "shadow fleet") use a whole toolkit to stay "untraceable and undeniable":

🏴
Flag hoppingAfter being struck from one registry, re-flag within days to loosely regulated states such as Sierra Leone, Comoros, or Cameroon.
🆔
Change name and MMSISever the link to old records. Some ships broadcast a name different from the one on the sanctions list — this name mismatch is itself a strong identity-concealment signal.
🔢
Fake IMO numberBroadcast a malformed (e.g. extra-digit) or fabricated IMO to defeat automated list-matching.
📡
Going dark & AIS spoofingSwitch off AIS during loading or in sensitive waters, or broadcast a false position.
🔄
Ship-to-ship (STS) transfersMove sanctioned oil to a "clean" ship on the open sea, cutting the cargo trail and avoiding port inspection.

為什麼台灣周邊海域看得到？對我們有什麼影響？

台灣海峽與周邊是東亞主要航道與海底電纜樞紐，船舶極為密集。受制裁的影子船隊油輪會在此過境、離岸待命，甚至做海上轉運。用公開制裁名單比對即時 AIS，就能發現多艘受 OFAC／EU／UANI 指定的油輪，此刻就在監測海域內——這是既成的官方指定，用 IMO 精確比對即可核實，並非臆測。這對台灣至少有四重影響：

🛢️
環境風險影子船隊多船齡老舊、無正規 P&I 保險。一旦在台灣海域碰撞或漏油，清污與賠償恐無人負責。
🚢
航行安全關閉 AIS、改名換旗、廣播假 IMO 的船，增加碰撞與搜救的困難。
🔌
海纜與基礎設施同一套隱匿與難以歸責的能力，也適用於在海纜密集區的灰色地帶活動。
🛰️
海域態勢與遵法制裁規避就發生在我們周邊，凸顯海域監控、港口國管制與資訊透明的重要。

Why Do They Appear in Taiwan's Waters — and Why Should We Care?

The Taiwan Strait and its surroundings are a major East Asian shipping lane and submarine-cable hub, with very dense traffic. Sanctioned shadow-fleet tankers transit, wait offshore, and even conduct transfers at sea here. Matching public sanctions lists against live AIS reveals several OFAC/EU/UANI-designated tankers inside the monitored area right now — an existing official designation, verifiable by exact IMO match, not speculation. This matters for Taiwan in at least four ways:

🛢️
Environmental riskShadow-fleet ships are often old and lack proper P&I insurance. A collision or spill in Taiwan's waters could leave clean-up and compensation with no responsible party.
🚢
Navigational safetyShips that switch off AIS, change names and flags, and broadcast fake IMOs make collision avoidance and search-and-rescue harder.
🔌
Cables & infrastructureThe same concealment and non-attribution capability also serves gray-zone activity in cable-dense waters.
🛰️
Domain awareness & complianceSanctions evasion happens right around us, underscoring the value of maritime monitoring, port-state control, and transparency.

本站如何判讀？

  1. 把多機構制裁名單（OFAC／EU／UK／UN／UANI／SECO／MFAT…）彙整成一份 IMO 對照集。
  2. 以船舶廣播的 IMO 精確比對——命中即標記為受制裁，並列出是哪些機構指定。
  3. 當 AIS 廣播的船名與制裁登記名不符時，額外標記為身分掩蓋。
  4. 不以船名比對大型名單（1400+ 常見船名易撞名），避免誤判。
  5. 結果只是比對事實——「上榜」是既成官方決定；本站不新增任何制裁認定。

How This Site Reads It

  1. Consolidate multi-agency sanctions lists (OFAC/EU/UK/UN/UANI/SECO/MFAT…) into a single IMO lookup set.
  2. Match a ship's broadcast IMO exactly — a hit is flagged as sanctioned, listing which agencies designated it.
  3. When the AIS-broadcast name differs from the sanctions-registered name, add an identity-concealment flag.
  4. Do not match a large list by ship name (1,400+ common names collide) to avoid false positives.
  5. The result is a fact-check — a listing is an existing official decision; this site adds no sanctions determination of its own.

資料限制與免責聲明

  • 制裁名單可能過時、缺漏或有同名異船；比對結果須回到官方來源核實。
  • 本站以 IMO 精確比對採信；純船名相符（無 IMO 佐證）信度低，不足以認定。
  • 「上榜」是各機構的官方決定，本站僅呈現公開名單的比對，不新增、不背書任何制裁認定。
  • 權宜旗、海上轉運、更換船名在許多情況下皆屬合法航運實務，不能單憑其一認定違法。

Data Limitations & Disclaimer

  • Sanctions lists can be stale, incomplete, or contain same-name/different-ship cases; matches must be verified against official sources.
  • This site relies on exact IMO matching; a name-only match without IMO corroboration is low-confidence and not a determination.
  • A listing is each agency's official decision; this site only shows a comparison against public lists — it adds and endorses no determination of its own.
  • Flags of convenience, transfers at sea, and name changes are lawful shipping practices in many cases; no single one proves wrongdoing.

FAQ

What are maritime sanctions?
Maritime sanctions are restrictions that governments and international bodies place on specific ships, owners, and shipping or insurance companies to cut off a target's shipping and money flows. A designated vessel is usually listed by its IMO number on public lists, and companies are barred from trading with it or providing insurance and port services.
Who can sanction a ship, and what are the lists?
The main ones are the U.S. Treasury's OFAC (SDN list), the EU, the UK's OFSI, the UN Security Council, Switzerland's SECO, and New Zealand's MFAT, plus NGO initiatives like UANI. One ship can be designated by several bodies at once; platforms like OpenSanctions consolidate these lists for lookup.
How do sanctioned ships evade?
Common tactics are re-flagging to loosely regulated flags of convenience, frequently changing names, broadcasting fake or invalid IMO numbers, switching off AIS in sensitive waters, and ship-to-ship (STS) transfers at sea to avoid port inspection. Because the IMO number is meant to be permanent, matching by IMO is most reliable — which is exactly the identifier ships most want to evade.
Why are sanctioned ships visible in Taiwan's waters?
The Taiwan Strait and its surroundings are a major East Asian shipping lane and cable hub with dense traffic. Sanctioned shadow-fleet tankers transit, wait, or transfer cargo here. Matching public sanctions lists against live AIS reveals several OFAC/EU/UANI-designated tankers inside the monitored area — not speculation, but the result of exact IMO matching.

參考資料與方法 Sources & Methodology

海事制裁屬高度事實導向主題。本頁的概念與比對訊號可對照以下公開權威來源驗證：

  • 制裁名單：美國 OFAC（SDN 名單）、歐盟 EU Sanctions Map、英國 OFSI 名單、聯合國安理會綜合名單。
  • 彙整平台：OpenSanctions（整合 OFAC／EU／UK／UN／烏克蘭等來源）、UANI（涉伊朗油輪）。
  • 船舶身分登記：ITU MARS（船台登記）、IMO（IMO 編號）。
  • 調查與分析報告：Lloyd's List Intelligence、RUSI、C4ADS。

本站制裁比對的資料來源、方式與性質：

  • UN 1718 清單：IMO 與船名比對；小型、事實核對。
  • 多機構影子船隊黑名單：1400+ 艘，僅以 IMO 精確比對（純船名易撞名）；命中即 事實比對，名單可能過時。
  • 身分掩蓋：IMO 命中但 AIS 廣播船名 ≠ 制裁登記名；推論訊號。
  • 權宜旗（FOC）：MMSI 的 MID 旗國對照；事實欄位，但合法常見。
本站不新增、不背書任何具名船舶、公司或國家的制裁認定；「上榜」是各機構的官方決定。所有比對結果均為待查線索，須回到上述官方來源核實，並非法律或事實認定。

Sources & Methodology

Maritime sanctions are a highly fact-driven topic. The concepts and matching signals here can be checked against these public, authoritative sources:

  • Sanctions lists: U.S. OFAC (SDN list), EU Sanctions Map, UK OFSI list, UN Security Council Consolidated List.
  • Aggregators: OpenSanctions (consolidates OFAC/EU/UK/UN/Ukraine and more), UANI (Iran-linked tankers).
  • Vessel identity registries: ITU MARS (ship stations), IMO (IMO number).
  • Investigative & analytical reporting: Lloyd's List Intelligence, RUSI, C4ADS.

The source, method, and nature of this site's sanctions matching:

  • UN 1718 list: matched by IMO and name; small, a fact-check.
  • Multi-agency shadow-fleet blacklist: 1,400+ vessels, matched by exact IMO only (bare names collide); a hit is a factual comparison, but lists can be stale.
  • Identity concealment: IMO hit but AIS-broadcast name ≠ sanctions-registered name; an inferred signal.
  • Flag of convenience (FOC): MID flag-state lookup from the MMSI; a factual field, but lawful and common.
This site adds and endorses no sanctions determination about any named vessel, company, or state; a listing is each agency's official decision. Every match is a lead to investigate that must be verified against the official sources above — not a legal or factual finding.
