本頁重點

In short

海域分區是依《聯合國海洋法公約》（UNCLOS），從領海基線向外海劃出的一系列範圍：領海（12 浬）、鄰接區（24 浬）、專屬經濟海域（200 浬）。離岸越遠，沿海國的權利越有限、他國的自由越大。本站地圖可疊上基線與 12／24 浬範圍，但這些線是近似的分析輔助，並非官方法律劃界。

Maritime zones are a series of bands measured seaward from a territorial baseline under UNCLOS: the territorial sea (12 nm), contiguous zone (24 nm), and exclusive economic zone (200 nm). The farther offshore, the more limited the coastal state's rights and the greater other states' freedoms. This site can overlay the baseline and 12/24 nm bands, but those lines are an approximate analysis aid, not an official legal delimitation.

什麼是海域分區與領海基線？

《聯合國海洋法公約》（UNCLOS）把海洋依「離岸距離」劃成數個法律地位不同的海域。一切的起點，是領海基線——通常沿用海岸的低潮線（正常基線），或在海岸破碎、外島眾多時，連接適當的點劃出直線基線。

關鍵在於：領海、鄰接區、專屬經濟海域的寬度，全都是從這條基線「向外海」量起，而不是從海岸線本身。越往外海，沿海國的權利越受限，他國的航行與鋪設電纜等自由則越大。

A ship sits 13 nautical miles off Taiwan's coast — just outside the territorial sea, yet well inside the contiguous zone. Is it "in Taiwan's waters"?

The answer depends on a few invisible lines, all drawn outward from a single baseline.

What Are Maritime Zones and the Territorial Baseline?

The UN Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS) divides the ocean by distance from shore into bands with different legal status. The starting point is the territorial baseline — usually the low-water line along the coast (the normal baseline), or, where the coast is broken or fringed with islands, straight baselines joining appropriate points.

The key point: the widths of the territorial sea, contiguous zone, and EEZ are all measured seaward from this baseline, not from the shoreline itself. The farther out, the more limited the coastal state's rights and the greater other states' freedoms of navigation and cable-laying.

四個海域，由內而外

0
領海基線（Baseline）所有寬度的起算線。本身不是一個「海域」，而是量測的基準。
12
領海（Territorial Sea，12 浬）沿海國主權所及，效力一如陸地領土；他國船舶僅享有「無害通過」權。
24
鄰接區（Contiguous Zone，24 浬）沿海國可對海關、財政、移民、衛生四類事項行使必要管制，以防止或處罰在領土或領海內的違法行為。
200
專屬經濟海域（EEZ，200 浬）沿海國對漁業、礦產等自然資源享有主權權利；但他國仍保有航行、飛越與鋪設海底電纜的自由。
一句話記住越靠近基線，沿海國「說了算」的程度越高；越往外海，越接近「公海自由」。鄰接區與 EEZ 不等於領土——船進入這些海域，多半仍屬合法。

Four Zones, From Inside Out

0
Territorial baselineThe line every width is measured from. Not a "zone" itself, but the measuring datum.
12
Territorial sea (12 nm)Under the coastal state's sovereignty, much like land territory; foreign ships have only the right of "innocent passage."
24
Contiguous zone (24 nm)The coastal state may exercise the control needed over customs, fiscal, immigration, and sanitary matters to prevent or punish breaches committed within its territory or territorial sea.
200
Exclusive economic zone (200 nm)The coastal state has sovereign rights over natural resources such as fisheries and minerals; but other states keep the freedoms of navigation, overflight, and submarine-cable laying.
One Line to RememberThe closer to the baseline, the more the coastal state has the final say; the farther out, the closer to "high-seas freedom." The contiguous zone and EEZ are not territory — a ship entering them is usually still acting lawfully.

這一點必須說清楚：本站繪製的基線與 12／24 浬範圍，是依公開資料近似計算的視覺化圖層，目的在於幫助理解與風險研判，不是官方法律劃界。

Legal Delimitation vs Analysis Aid

This must be said plainly: the baseline and 12/24 nm bands drawn here are an approximate visualization computed from public data, meant to aid understanding and risk assessment — not an official legal delimitation.

Real maritime boundaries involve precise basepoint coordinates, agreements and disputes between neighboring states, and are usually fixed by official charts and treaties. This site's lines may differ from official boundaries and imply no sovereignty claim or border position. Treat them as a "reference grid to think with," not a "legal boundary marker."

為什麼與灰色地帶相關？

海域分區之所以重要，是因為船舶在哪個海域、做了什麼，決定了行為的法律意涵與可疑程度。外國船在公海航行稀鬆平常；但若在領海內無故低速徘徊、或在鄰接區與電纜路線附近反覆停留、又適逢 AIS 關閉，警戒層級就完全不同。

這也是灰色地帶手法的精妙之處：它常刻意游走在這些界線的模糊地帶，既不明顯越界，又持續製造壓力。把船舶軌跡與海域範圍疊圖，正是本站把「位置」轉化為「風險脈絡」的方式。

Why It Matters for the Gray Zone

Maritime zones matter because where a ship is, and what it does there, determine the legal meaning and suspiciousness of its behavior. A foreign ship transiting the high seas is unremarkable; but loitering at low speed inside the territorial sea, or lingering repeatedly in the contiguous zone near a cable route with AIS switched off, is a completely different alert level.

That is the cleverness of gray-zone tactics: they often deliberately work the blurry edges of these lines, never plainly crossing over yet continuously applying pressure. Overlaying vessel tracks with the zones is exactly how this site turns "position" into "risk context."

常見問題 FAQ

什麼是領海基線？
領海基線是測量各海域寬度的起算線。通常沿用海岸低潮線（正常基線），或在海岸破碎、有外島時，連接適當點劃出直線基線。所有海域的寬度，都是從這條基線「向外海算」的。
領海、鄰接區、專屬經濟海域有什麼不同？
領海（12 浬）是沿海國主權所及的海域；鄰接區（24 浬）內沿海國可對海關、財政、移民與衛生事項行使必要管制；專屬經濟海域（200 浬）賦予沿海國對自然資源的主權權利，但他國仍享有航行與鋪設電纜的自由。
本站地圖上的領海／鄰接區範圍是官方劃界嗎？
不是。本站的基線與 12／24 浬範圍是依公開資料近似繪製，供視覺化與風險分析參考，並非官方法律劃界，也不代表任何主權或邊界主張。
海域分區和灰色地帶監測有什麼關係？
船舶位於哪個海域，會影響其行為的法律意涵與可疑程度。外國船在領海內長時間徘徊、或在鄰接區／電纜路線附近異常停留，比在公海航行更值得警戒。本站把船舶與海域範圍疊圖，作為威脅研判的輔助線索。

FAQ

What is a territorial baseline?
The territorial baseline is the line from which each zone's width is measured. It usually follows the coast's low-water line (the normal baseline), or joins appropriate points as straight baselines where the coast is broken or has offshore islands. Every zone's width is measured seaward from this baseline.
What's the difference between the territorial sea, contiguous zone, and EEZ?
The territorial sea (12 nm) is under the coastal state's sovereignty; within the contiguous zone (24 nm) the state may exercise the control needed over customs, fiscal, immigration, and sanitary matters; the EEZ (200 nm) gives the state sovereign rights over natural resources, while other states keep the freedoms of navigation and cable-laying.
Are the zones on this site's map official boundaries?
No. The baseline and 12/24 nm bands are drawn approximately from public data for visualization and risk analysis. They are not an official legal delimitation and imply no sovereignty or border claim.
How do maritime zones relate to gray-zone monitoring?
Which zone a vessel is in shapes the legal meaning and suspiciousness of its behavior. A foreign ship loitering inside the territorial sea, or lingering anomalously in the contiguous zone or near a cable route, warrants more caution than one transiting the high seas. This site overlays vessels with the zones as a supporting lead for threat assessment.
