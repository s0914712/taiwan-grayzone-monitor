想像這樣一個規則：所有在公路上行駛的車輛，每隔幾秒鐘就必須向全世界廣播自己的車牌號碼、目前位置、速度和目的地。

這正是海洋上的規則——針對大型船隻。這個系統叫做 AIS。直到有人偷偷把廣播器關掉。

AIS：船舶的數位護照

AIS（Automatic Identification System，自動識別系統）是一種裝設在船隻上的無線電應答器，透過 VHF 超短波自動廣播船舶資訊。根據國際海事組織（IMO）的 SOLAS 公約規定，300 總噸以上的遠洋船舶及所有客輪，都必須裝設並啟動 AIS 系統。

AIS 每隔幾秒鐘廣播一次，讓附近的其他船隻、港口管理當局、海巡單位，乃至全球衛星網路，都能追蹤這艘船的即時位置。

Imagine this rule: every vehicle on the highway must broadcast its license plate number, current position, speed, and destination to the entire world every few seconds.

That's exactly the rule at sea — for large vessels. The system is called AIS. Until someone secretly switches off the broadcaster.

AIS: The Ship's Digital Passport

AIS (Automatic Identification System) is a radio transponder installed on vessels that automatically broadcasts ship information via VHF radio. Under the IMO's SOLAS convention, all ocean-going vessels over 300 gross tons and all passenger ships are required to have AIS installed and activated.

AIS broadcasts every few seconds, allowing nearby vessels, port authorities, coast guards, and global satellite networks to track the ship's real-time position.

AIS 的致命弱點：任何人都能關掉它

AIS 系統的最大問題在於，它是完全自願廣播的——沒有人能強迫一艘船保持訊號開啟。任何船員都可以在幾秒鐘內按下幾個按鈕，讓這艘船從所有 AIS 追蹤平台上消失。

這種行為在業界稱為「走暗」（going dark）。走暗並不一定違法——某些合法情況下，船長有理由關閉 AIS，例如在海盜出沒區域保護安全。但在台灣周邊海域，一艘船在靠近海底電纜時突然關閉 AIS，那就是一個非常值得警戒的信號。

除了關閉，AIS 還可以被偽造——船隻可以廣播假的位置座標、虛報速度，甚至播送其他船隻的身分。這種「AIS 欺騙」（AIS Spoofing）是我們評分系統中特別關注的項目。

AIS's Fatal Flaw: Anyone Can Turn It Off

The biggest problem with AIS is that it's entirely voluntary broadcasting — nobody can force a ship to keep its signal on. Any crew member can make a vessel disappear from all AIS tracking platforms in a few button presses.

This is called "going dark" in the industry. Going dark isn't necessarily illegal — there are legitimate reasons for a captain to disable AIS, such as for safety in piracy-prone areas. But in Taiwan's surrounding waters, a vessel suddenly disabling AIS while approaching a submarine cable route is a very serious warning signal.

Beyond switching off, AIS can also be spoofed — vessels can broadcast false position coordinates, fake speeds, or even another vessel's identity. This "AIS Spoofing" is an item our scoring system specifically monitors.

為什麼船隻會「走暗」？

🐟
非法漁業希望隱藏越界捕魚或在禁漁區作業的行為，避免被漁業管理單位追蹤。
🛢️
制裁規避受到國際制裁的國家（如俄羅斯、北韓、伊朗）透過「影子船隊」轉運物資，關閉 AIS 以隱匿貨物流向。
🔄
海上旁靠（STS）在公海進行船對船貨物轉運，刻意關閉 AIS 以避免港口檢查，常見於石油走私。
🔌
海底電纜破壞接近海底電纜前關閉 AIS，目的是在執行破壞行動時無法被即時追蹤與攔截——這正是 2025 年 1 月「興順 39 號」的模式。
🔍
海上偵察情報蒐集船隻在敏感海域活動時關閉 AIS，避免暴露偵察意圖。
本站的觀察：2025 年 1 月 11 日，「興順 39 號」在距離台亞美（TAW-US）海底電纜約 10 公里處關閉 AIS，之後被確認已切斷電纜。這種「接近電纜前走暗」的模式，正是本站威脅評分系統著重偵測的行為之一。

SAR 衛星：黑暗中的眼睛

AIS 走暗並不代表船隻消失了——只是消失在一般的追蹤系統中。合成孔徑雷達（SAR, Synthetic Aperture Radar）衛星可以透過雷達掃描發現任何在水面上的金屬物體，完全不受天氣、雲層或日夜影響。

本站使用的 SAR 資料來自 Global Fishing Watch（全球漁業觀察），他們透過 SAR 衛星在台灣周邊海域進行掃描，將偵測到的船隻位置與 AIS 資料進行交叉比對：

  • SAR 偵測到的船 + 有 AIS 訊號 → 正常
  • SAR 偵測到的船 + 無 AIS 訊號 → 暗船（Dark Vessel）

這些「暗船」就成為本站優先監測的對象。一艘在海底電纜路線附近被 SAR 偵測到、卻沒有 AIS 訊號的船隻，立刻引發高度警覺。

SAR Satellites: Eyes in the Dark

Going dark on AIS doesn't mean a vessel has disappeared — only that it's vanished from conventional tracking systems. Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) satellites can detect any metallic object on the water surface via radar scanning, completely unaffected by weather, cloud cover, or time of day.

The SAR data used by this site comes from Global Fishing Watch, which scans Taiwan's surrounding waters via SAR satellites and cross-references detected vessel positions against AIS data:

  • SAR-detected vessel + has AIS signal → Normal
  • SAR-detected vessel + no AIS signal → Dark Vessel

These "dark vessels" become priority monitoring targets. A vessel detected by SAR near a submarine cable route with no AIS signal immediately triggers high-level alertness.

身分操弄：比走暗更狡猾

有些船隻更進一步——不只關閉 AIS，還頻繁更換船名、呼號甚至虛報 IMO 號碼，製造身分混淆。這種行為本身就是一個重大警訊。

影子船隊的身分操弄手法 根據 dd.md 研究報告的案例分析，典型的影子船隻會：
  • 在不同港口間頻繁更換船旗（如從坦尚尼亞換到多哥再換到喀麥隆）
  • 更改船名，讓舊的追蹤記錄難以與新名稱連結
  • 透過海外空殼公司層層隱匿實際船主
  • 進行海上旁靠（STS）以轉移貨物，避免進港被查驗
本站的「身分追蹤」功能，專門偵測這類頻繁更換身分的異常行為。

Identity Manipulation: More Cunning Than Going Dark

Some vessels go further — not just disabling AIS, but frequently changing their name, call sign, or even falsely reporting their IMO number to create identity confusion. This behavior itself is a major red flag.

Shadow Fleet Identity Manipulation Tactics Based on case analysis in our research, typical shadow vessels will:
  • Frequently change flags between ports (e.g., Tanzania → Togo → Cameroon)
  • Change vessel names to disconnect old tracking records from new identities
  • Conceal actual ship owners through layers of offshore shell companies
  • Conduct ship-to-ship (STS) transfers to move cargo, avoiding port inspections
This site's Identity Tracking feature is specifically designed to detect such frequent identity-change anomalies.

AIS 與海底電纜：把所有線索連起來

現在我們可以看清楚這條邏輯鏈：

  1. AIS 讓我們知道每艘大型船隻在哪裡、在做什麼
  2. 當船隻「走暗」或進行 AIS 欺騙，我們透過 SAR 衛星找回它
  3. 我們將船隻的位置與台灣海底電纜路線進行比對
  4. 如果一艘船在電纜附近走暗、低速徘徊、或呈現異常航行模式，威脅評分就會升高
  5. 評分達到門檻的船隻被標記為「可疑」，列入本站監測清單

這套邏輯就是本站監測系統的核心。想了解完整的評分方法，請參閱第 06 篇：本網站所使用之方法。

AIS and Submarine Cables: Connecting All the Dots

Now we can see the clear logical chain:

  1. AIS tells us where every large vessel is and what it's doing
  2. When a vessel "goes dark" or uses AIS spoofing, we find it via SAR satellites
  3. We cross-reference vessel positions against Taiwan's submarine cable routes
  4. If a vessel goes dark near a cable, loiters at low speed, or shows anomalous navigation patterns, its threat score rises
  5. Vessels reaching the score threshold are flagged as "suspicious" and listed on this site

This logic is the core of our monitoring system. For the full scoring methodology, see Article 06: Our Methodology.
